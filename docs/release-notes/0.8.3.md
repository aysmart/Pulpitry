Say where you want to be, and the screen goes there.

## References, spoken

"John nine eight", "John nine verse eight", "John chapter nine verse eight" —
all the same request, and all of them work. So do numbered books however the
recogniser wrote them: "first John four eight" and "one john four eight" are
both 1 John 4:8.

Book names come from the translation you are reading, so a Bible that does not
have a book simply does not move — and a Catholic edition's extra books can be
asked for by name like any other.

## Chapters

"Next chapter", "previous chapter", "chapter four", "chapter four verse nine",
"next chapter verse nine", and "verse nine" on its own, which keeps the chapter
you are in.

A chapter the book does not have is ignored rather than clamped: "chapter
ninety" in a book with five is a misheard number, and going to the last chapter
instead would look like it worked.

## What the screen does

An instruction moves what is staged and what is on the screen, not just the
chapter list — under the same rule Next Verse has always used. If what it
replaced was live, the new one is live; if you were only previewing, it stays a
preview. Speaking never puts something in front of a congregation that you had
not already put there.

## Fixed

A licence key applied in Settings now takes effect immediately. Pressing Start
Listening straight afterwards used to send you back to the account page, over
and over, until you happened to change the transcription engine and back.

## Only a finished sentence counts

An instruction is acted on when you stop speaking, not while the words are
still arriving. "Next chapter" is a complete instruction on the way to "next
chapter verse nine", and acting on both moved two chapters for one thing said
once. The same was true of "the King James" on the way to "the New King James".
