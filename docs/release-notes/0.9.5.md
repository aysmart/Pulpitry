The screen stops following chapters the preacher only mentioned, and starts
following the verses they actually read.

## A chapter named on its own no longer goes to air

"Turn with me to John chapter three" used to put verse 1 on the screen in
front of the congregation. So did a chapter mentioned on the way to
somewhere else, as a cross-reference, or as where you were last week — and
each one pulled the display away from what was being read.

A chapter named without a verse now goes to **Preview and the scripture
pane only**. You can see where the preacher is heading while the screen
keeps the verse they are on.

## And the verse they call goes out

The other half, which never worked: a preacher says the book once and then
reads by number for the next twenty minutes. "Verse sixteen" names no book,
so it was heard as nothing at all and the announcement was the last thing
the screen ever saw.

The announced chapter is now remembered for five minutes — long enough for
a reading, an explanation and a prayer — and a verse called inside it goes
to air like any reference you had spoken in full. Working down a chapter
follows the verse just called.

The word "verse" is required. A sermon is full of numbers — years, ages,
"three things this morning" — and a screen that moved for any of them would
be switched off inside one service.

## A chapter your translation has not got

Naming one used to stop detection for that whole sentence, so anything said
in the same breath was lost with it. It is now simply not found, and the
rest of the sentence is still read.

## Also

* Announcing a second chapter replaces the first, so a verse called after
  it belongs to the new passage.
