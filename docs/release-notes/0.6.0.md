## Where you are reading follows what is staged

Clicking a verse in the Detected panel put it on screen and left the Scripture
panel wherever it happened to be. Now the book, the chapter, the verse **and the
translation** all follow it — from a detection, from the queue, and from Prev and
Next. The verse is scrolled to and lit in the chapter list.

It matters most beside the Original panel. That shows the Greek or Hebrew for
whatever is staged, so working through a detected verse meant hunting for it in
the chapter list by hand while the sermon moved on. The two now look at the same
place by construction.

The translation follows what is *read*, not what is *heard*. Verse detection
searches one translation, so a panel that switched it whenever you clicked a
match in another version would quietly retarget detection mid-sermon — one click
on a Yoruba match during an English service and the English stops being found.
Your working translation changes when you change it from the picker, and never as
a side effect of clicking a verse.

And it only moves when a *new* verse is staged. If you deliberately browse to
Romans while John 3:16 is live, you are left where you went.
