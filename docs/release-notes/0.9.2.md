## The highlight and the list move together

A list is scrolled by you and its selection is moved by the app: Next verse,
"next verse" said aloud, a detection staging a passage, a deck stepping on. The
highlight moved and the list stayed where it was, so the verse on the screen was
marked somewhere below the fold and you had to go looking for the place you
already were.

Now the list follows. Everywhere a selection can move from outside the list:

- the chapter, including a step into the next chapter
- detections, and the History and Queue feed
- the song library, a song's parts, and the song set
- the slide library, and the strip of slides along the top of the slide desk
- pictures and clips, the service plan, and Song Finder

It follows whichever moved — what is live or what is staged. It scrolls only as
far as it needs to, so something already on screen does not move and nothing
jumps to the top. And it acts only when the selection changes: scroll ahead to
read what is coming and the list stays where you put it.
