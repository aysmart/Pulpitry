A long service is summarised without losing the work when the model gets busy.

## Waiting, instead of starting over

A four-hour service is read in several stretches — one request each, and one
more to draw them together. On an ordinary deployment that is enough requests in
enough seconds to exhaust its per-minute quota, and 0.7.6 gave up when it did.

A 27,000-word service got through all three stretches and was refused on the
last request of the four. Every stretch had been summarised; all of it was
thrown away. Trying again did the same thing, because trying again immediately
is the one thing that cannot work.

Pulpitry now waits and carries on. Where the service says how long to wait, it
waits exactly that long. If the quota has not cleared within about half a
minute, it says so plainly rather than sitting there — at that point the
deployment is too small for the length of service being asked of it, and that is
worth knowing.
