The Song Finder's catalogue has more than doubled, and Pulpitry will now offer it to you.

## 23,938 songs

The catalogue behind the AI Song Finder has grown from 10,272 songs to 23,938
— worship, hymnody and gospel from 658 artists, in English, Portuguese,
Spanish, Yoruba and more.

## And a way to actually get it

The catalogue is downloaded once and searched on your own machine after that,
which is what makes it instant and what makes it work with the network
unplugged. It is also why, until now, nothing ever told you the catalogue had
grown: the download appeared only when you had no catalogue at all, so a
church that pulled it once kept whatever it first got — for ever.

Pulpitry now asks what is available when you open the Song Finder, and if
there is more, says so above the results:

> **13,666 more songs are available — 23,938 in all.**  *Update*

Nothing downloads until you press it. Dismissing the notice lasts for that
session, not for good.

If the check cannot reach the network it says nothing at all: what is on your
machine still works, and an error about a question you did not ask is not
something to read during a service.
