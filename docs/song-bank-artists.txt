# Derived from gospel_artists.txt by keeping only those whose catalogue
# actually is Christian: of their songs in the scrape, at least 70% name
# God in some language (60% for an artist with fewer than twenty songs,
# where the share is too noisy to cut hard on).
#
# That test exists because gospel_artists.txt mixes acts whose whole
# catalogue is worship with artists who merely recorded some gospel --
# Ella Fitzgerald, Dolly Parton, Aretha Franklin -- and the scraper took
# everything by anyone on it.
#
# It reads a catalogue through an English-leaning word list, so an artist
# singing in a language it barely covers scores low however plainly
# Christian they are. Soweto Gospel Choir came out at 35% and is here by
# hand. Expect more of those; they belong in this file, not in a cleverer
# threshold.
#
